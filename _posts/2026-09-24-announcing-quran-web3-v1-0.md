## ✨ Features You Can Explore Right Now in V1.0

The stable V1.0 release brings a premium, high-fidelity reading and listening experience directly to your browser, built with your daily worship in mind:

### 1. Replicated Quran.com Experience & Deep Study Tools
To make you feel instantly at home, the interface mirrors the clean, beautiful layout of Quran.com. 
*   **Two Reading Modes:** Switch between **Surah Mode** for continuous recitation and **Page Mode** for deep, focused study.
*   **Renowned Reciters:** Listen to high-definition audio from 6 world-renowned Qaris (including Al-Sudais, Al-Shatri, Fares Abbad, Al-Muaiqly, Al-Ghamdi, and Al-Shuraim) to perfect your pronunciation.
*   **Rich Context:** Access beautiful color-coded Tajweed rules, interactive word-by-word translations, and the complete Tafsir Ibn Kathir instantly.
*   **Global Access:** Read the meaning of the Quran in 12 different international languages.

### 2. The "Quran in a Year" Algorithmic Khatm Tracker
Want to complete the entire Quran but find it hard to stay consistent? Our automated **Khatm Calculator** divides the Quran into highly manageable, bite-sized daily targets. On average, it asks for just 2 pages a day, automatically advancing your target every midnight to help you build a lasting spiritual habit over 365 days.

### 3. Memorization Companion (Verse Repeater)
For students of knowledge and those memorizing the Quran (Hifz), our *Verse Repeater* lets you select an exact starting verse, ending verse, and the number of times you want the audio to loop. It acts as an offline digital companion to lock verses into your memory.

### 4. Advanced "Karaoke-Style" Visual Audio Fill
When you press play, a smooth background color dynamically fills the Arabic text, tracking the progress of the audio in real-time. This high-performance feature was built mathematically to run smoothly on any device without draining your battery or slowing down your browser.

## 🔒 Absolute Privacy & True Data Ownership

The defining feature of Quran Web3 is that **you are in total control.** 
*   **No Accounts, No Tracking:** There are no sign-ups, no email requirements, and absolutely zero third-party tracking scripts (such as Google Analytics). Your spiritual journey remains completely private between you and Allah.
*   **Decentralized Data Center:** Because there is no central database, your bookmarks, personal reading notes, and Khatm progress are saved securely **only on your own device's local storage.** 
*   **Full Data Portability:** You can easily download your entire reading state as a secure `.json` backup file and upload it into any other device to pick up right where you left off.

## 🛡️ Built-in Content Guard

Because the files on a decentralized network are open-source, anyone can copy them. To protect the global Muslim community from fake clones that might secretly alter the holy text or inject malicious tracking codes, the platform includes a built-in **Cryptographic Security Guard**. The moment our script detects that someone is using an unofficial, tampered copy, it automatically halts execution and safely redirects the user back to our verified official domain.
